# Dependency resolution - `T##-??` placeholders

Status: first pass, committed with the card edits it applies. Generated by hand from the cards, the 04/03/14A export blocks and the merged code; every row below was checked against the card that declares the symbol.

## What was measured

`python export/build-plan/tools/order.py` on `main` before this branch:

```
401 tasks ordered; 306 problems      (305 unknown dependency + 1 pre-existing cycle)
```

305 placeholder edges across 142 of the 401 task cards, in 34 distinct placeholders (the earlier "152 of 429" counted `_unit.md` files as cards; 429 files = 401 cards + 28 unit files).

After this branch:

```
401 tasks ordered; 253 problems      (252 unknown dependency + the same cycle)
```

**53 placeholder rows resolved into 84 real edges; 306 -> 253 problems (-53).** The problem count falls by exactly one per resolved placeholder; the edge count rises because one placeholder can honestly need two or three cards.

## The rule, and where it stops

1. A placeholder is resolved **only** when the card that ships the named symbol is unambiguous, and the symbol and the card are named in the table below.
2. **One placeholder may become a list.** Where a need genuinely spans several cards - three 05 cards needing `registerEntity`, one reason code and one more - the table names all of them. Over-depending orders a card later; it never orders it too early.
3. **No symbol annotation, no resolution.** 154 of the 305 rows have no `- T##-??:` bullet in `## Imports` at all. Nothing in the card says what it needs, so nothing can be matched. These are authored gaps, listed in full in the last table.
4. **A missing target card is not resolved to a neighbour.** Where the describing card does not exist (T04-20, the whole of T21 and T18), the placeholder stays. Resolving it to a card that does not ship the symbol would silently mis-order the build and nobody re-checks a resolved edge.

## Findings, in order of how much they block

### 1. `T04-20` does not exist, and 25 of the 32 `T04-??` edges need it

The 04 unit on disk is 19 cards (`T04-01`, `T04-01b`, `T04-02` .. `T04-18`). Its own cards cite `T04-20` **31 times across 15 cards** as the macro read API - `T04-03:67` says "05 reads `getBlocRegime` (T04-20 api)", `T04-09:84` says "05 reads them with T04-20 `npcPoolOrders(store, tick)`", `T04-16:70` "T04-20's `factorJumps(store, tick)`", `T04-18:93` "05 reads through T04-20 `macroValueShocks`". `T04-21` (module assembly and reason-code registration) is cited 11 times across 8 cards and is also absent. `T04-19` is not cited at all.

Consequence, measured, not inferred: `getCurves`, `getGlobalMacro`, `getFxState`, `getBlocRegime`, `priceIndex`, `outputGap`, `y10`, `cpiAt`, `neutralRate`, `policyRate`, `npcPoolOrders`, `scheduledMandateFlows`, `factorJumps`, `macroValueShocks`, `marketHookCalls`, `issuanceCalendar`, `billYield`, `govYield` and `fear` appear in **no card of unit 04**, and none of them exists in `packages/engine/macro` either (it holds `blocs.ts`, `events.ts`, `gapest.ts`, `ids.ts` only).

So 25 of the 32 `T04-??` edges cannot be resolved by matching symbols - the target card has to be written first. That single card is the highest-leverage fix in the plan: it unblocks the T04 group and, through `getCurves`/`getGlobalMacro`, most of 06A and 06B as well.

### 3. A pre-existing cycle holds 322 of 401 cards, and it is not caused by the placeholders

The tool reports one `CYCLE` line. The graph says it is two components: `{T02-01, T02-04, T22A-01}` and one **319-card component** containing everything from 03 to 20B. The closing edges are:

- `T22A-01` depends on `T02-01` and `T02-04`, while unit 02 is declared after 22A (`order.py` `UNIT_DEPS`).
- `T03-29` depends on `T20B-02`, `T20B-06`, `T20B-07`, `T20B-08`, `T20B-09`, `T20B-11`, and `T14B-14` depends on `T20B-02`, while `UNIT_DEPS["20B"]` puts 20B after every other unit.

Measured before and after this branch: identical, 322 cards in cycles both times. Until those two edges are cut, the 319-card component is emitted in arbitrary order, so the "sequence" does not exist for most of the plan even with every placeholder resolved. Fixing it is a plan decision (drop the 20B dependency from T03-29, or split 20B), not a symbol match, so this branch does not touch it.
